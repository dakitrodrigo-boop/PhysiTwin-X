INTERPRETATION NOTES
SHAP values explain how transformed input features contribute to the model output for each class.
Positive SHAP values increase the model output for the corresponding class; negative values decrease it.
Mean absolute SHAP values measure the average magnitude of contributions, not their direction.
SHAP explanations describe model behavior and do not establish causation or prove future spoilage.
The original risk labels may be dependent on firmness and peel-color observations used as predictors.
Therefore, feature contributions must be interpreted with the label-dependency assessment in Step 10D.
